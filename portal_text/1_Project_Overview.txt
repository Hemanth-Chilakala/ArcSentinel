ArcSentinel: FPGA-Accelerated Detection of Downed and Arcing Power Lines

Problem: When a live distribution conductor falls onto dry grass, sand, asphalt or a tree, it often draws too little current for conventional overcurrent relays to detect. These high-impedance faults (HIFs) keep arcing while the line stays energised, igniting wildfires and electrocuting people and livestock. Power lines were implicated in some of the deadliest wildfires on record, including the 2018 Camp Fire and the 2023 Lahaina fire.

Solution: ArcSentinel is a real-time feeder-protection unit built on the PolarFire SoC Icicle Kit. A Microchip MCP3914B simultaneous-sampling ADC digitises feeder voltages and currents at 25.6 kS/s. A deterministic fabric pipeline, built with SmartHLS and CoreFFT, extracts harmonic, wavelet, asymmetry and cycle-to-cycle randomness features every power cycle. An int8 neural network in fabric then separates HIFs from six look-alike events: capacitor switching, transformer inrush, welders, nonlinear loads, load steps and normal operation. A bare-metal RISC-V hart (AMP) applies security logic and alarms or trips within 0.5 s. Hard-wired overcurrent protection in fabric keeps working even if software fails. Linux on the remaining harts publishes IEC 61850 GOOSE trip messages over both Gigabit Ethernet ports using PRP zero-recovery redundancy, stores COMTRADE fault records and serves a web HMI.

Target application: Distribution substations and pole-top reclosers for utilities, rural co-operatives, microgrids, solar farms and mines in wildfire-prone or densely populated regions.

Expected outcome: A working Icicle Kit prototype, validated on public field-test data and by hardware-in-the-loop replay, demonstrating at least 95% HIF detection, zero false trips on the look-alike set, and sub-millisecond fabric overcurrent tripping, with latency, jitter and energy measured against a software-only version on the same RISC-V cores. Our pre-proposal model already detects 98% of synthetic HIFs, including sub-ampere arcs, versus 3% for a conventional relay with inrush restraint.
